Care & Maintenance

1. Always Hand Wash & Dry Immediately
Never place high-carbon or Damascus steel knives in a dishwasher. Wash gently with warm soapy water and dry completely with a soft cloth to prevent patina or oxidation.
2. Sharpening via Water Whetstones
Maintain your blade using 1000/6000 grit Japanese whetstones. Avoid pull-through carbide sharpeners, which ruin edge geometry and remove excessive steel.
3. Proper Cutting Board Surface
Always use end-grain wood or soft plastic cutting boards. Hard surfaces like glass, marble, or ceramic will instantly dull and chip fine edges.
